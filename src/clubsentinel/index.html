The problem

It's not one logbook. It's a dozen, in a dozen places.

Greenkeeping's records are in the shed, the kitchen's are by the pass, the cellar checks are on a clipboard and the training certificates sit in someone's inbox. You're accountable for all of it — and on any given day, nobody's quite sure what's current. ClubSentinel brings every duty into one system, captured where the work happens.

How it works

Two ways in. One system that answers back.

Speak a record from the course, or hand ClubSentinel the documents you already have — the AI reads them, builds the records, and answers your questions from them.

01

Speak it, from where the work happens

A greenkeeper describes the day's checks from the course; a manager logs an incident by voice. AI turns the words into a structured, dated record — no blank forms.

02

Upload it, and the AI reads it

Drop in a safety data sheet, a certificate or a policy PDF and ClubSentinel pulls the fields straight out — populating your COSHH register, training matrix and document store.

03

Ask it, in plain words

"What's still outstanding before Friday's inspection?" Get a straight answer drawn only from your club's records — no digging through folders or inboxes.

04

It joins the dots

One voice note about a chemical spill can log the incident, update the COSHH register, raise the maintenance fault and prep the RIDDOR check — all linked, entered once.

Questions clubs ask

The honest answers, before you ask.

Is ClubSentinel built specifically for golf clubs?

Yes. The modules, language and workflows are built around how UK golf clubs actually run — the greenkeeping shed, the course, the clubhouse kitchen and the bar cellar — not generic compliance software bent to fit. The Greens Report module in particular has no equivalent in general H&S tools.

How does the live compliance score work?

Your overall score is a single number from 0–100, weighted across seven core areas: Incidents (22%), Maintenance (18%), Training (16%), Documents (13%), Inspections (12%), COSHH (12%) and Assets (7%). Each area scores on whether records are present, current and complete, and the total moves as records go in. A "this week's focus" line tells you your lowest area and the single most useful thing to do next.

Do I have to type everything in to get started?

No — that's the part most systems get wrong. You populate ClubSentinel from documents you already have: upload a safety data sheet and the AI reads it straight into your COSHH register; upload certificates and policies and they're filed and dated automatically. After setup, keeping it current is a few seconds a day by voice.

Can the whole team use it, including volunteers?

Yes. Unlimited staff accounts are included at no extra cost, so greenkeepers, bar staff, the club manager and committee members can all log and view what's relevant to them. There's no per-user fee.

Does it cover our HSE obligations?

ClubSentinel is built around UK health & safety duties — risk assessments, COSHH, RIDDOR reporting, training records, equipment checks and the evidence trail an inspector or insurer expects. It helps you stay inspection-ready and prove what you've done, though it doesn't replace your own legal responsibility for compliance.

Is there a contract, and what does it cost?

£199 per month for one club — every module, the live compliance score, unlimited staff accounts and onboarding included. No tiers and no per-user fees. Billed monthly with no long contract, and you can cancel anytime. Annual invoicing is available on request.
