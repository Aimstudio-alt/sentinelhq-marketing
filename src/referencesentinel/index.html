Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is ReferenceSentinel?

An automated employment reference checking platform for UK recruitment agencies. You send a request, your candidate nominates their referees, each referee completes a structured questionnaire, and the results land in your dashboard with AI summaries, three-layer fraud detection and a printable reference pack.

How does pricing work?

There are two ways to pay. The Compliance Suite is one monthly plan per agency, covering referencing plus workforce compliance (Right to Work and DBS tracking, training vault, readiness scoring and a shift booking compliance gate), with reference credits included every month and unlimited users. Pay as you go is referencing only: buy credits when you need them, with no subscription. One credit covers one candidate and up to five referees, and credits never expire. We don't publish a fixed price list, so book a demo and we'll quote a rate that fits your volume.

What happens if a referee never responds?

You're not charged for incomplete checks. Credits are automatically refunded if a candidate doesn't nominate referees within 14 days, or a referee doesn't respond within 21 days. Automated reminders nudge things along, and an email bounce triggers an instant refund.

How does the fraud detection work?

Every submission runs through three independent checks automatically: AI stylometric analysis, same-IP detection and Companies House employer verification, alongside rule-based checks like employment-date gaps. Anything suspicious is surfaced clearly, and you can dismiss false positives or override AI assessments with a full audit trail.

Can I customise the reference questions?

Yes. Build question profiles tailored to a role or sector, or let the AI generate one from a job description in seconds. Care-sector profiles are CQC-aligned and included as standard.

Is candidate and referee data secure?

Data is handled in line with UK data protection law. ReferenceSentinel is operated by SentinelHQ Limited (ICO registered ZC175485), with referee and candidate journeys protected by secure, expiring access tokens and UK data residency.
