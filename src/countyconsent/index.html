The problem

The risks on every junior golf trip

Taking juniors away is one of the best parts of golf. But the paperwork behind it carries risks most clubs and unions haven't fully considered.

Real risk
🚑

Medical emergency on a trip

"What if a young golfer has a medical emergency and the consent form, with their allergy information and GP number, is back at the office?"

Legal risk
📄

No audit trail to prove consent

"What if a parent disputes they ever gave consent, and you have no digital record, no timestamp, and no signature to prove they did?"

Compliance risk
🛡️

ICO investigation readiness

"What if the ICO asks to see your data handling records and you're running on paper forms, shared email threads and spreadsheets?"

Features

Everything your organisation needs, nothing it doesn't

Purpose-built for junior golf. No generic safeguarding software shoehorned to fit, designed for exactly this use case.

🛡️

Secure digital consent forms

Every parent gets a unique, tamper-proof link. No account needed, complete on any device in under 3 minutes.

➕

Medical alerts

Critical health information is instantly visible to staff, allergies, conditions, medication, exactly when needed.

📋

Full audit trail

Every action logged, timestamped and exportable. Know exactly who consented, when, and from which device.

✅

GDPR compliant

UK/EU data storage, ICO-ready reporting and full special-category data handling, built to the standard from day one.

⏰

Consent expiry tracking

Automatic alerts when consent is approaching renewal. Never take a junior away on outdated consent again.

🔒

Role-based access

Staff see only what they need. Medical data is protected, accessible only to those who need it on the day.

👥

Junior golfer register

Every junior in one place, name, club, age, consent status and medical flags at a glance across all trips.

🎂

GDPR age-18 alerts

Automatic alerts when a junior turns 18, with a guided workflow to erase their data in line with UK GDPR minimisation rules.

📊

Live dashboard

The dashboard refreshes automatically as consent forms arrive, no manual refresh during the busy run-up to a trip.

FAQ

Questions answered

Is there a setup fee?

No. There are no setup fees, no onboarding fees, and no hidden costs of any kind. Your subscription covers everything.

How many staff users can we have?

Unlimited. Add as many coaches, administrators and county staff as you need, your subscription covers your entire organisation.

Where is our data stored?

Your data is stored in UK/EU regions. Where a provider we use processes data outside the UK/EEA, that transfer is covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or Standard Contractual Clauses. Our sub-processors are listed in our privacy notice.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel at any time and your subscription will run until the end of the current monthly billing period.

Is it really built for golf organisations?

Yes. CountyConsent was built in direct partnership with Durham County Golf Union and is designed for both county unions and individual golf clubs. It reflects the real workflows, safeguarding requirements and England Golf accreditation standards that organisations face.

Can I see the platform before signing up?

Yes. You can try the interactive demo on our website, or request a guided walkthrough with our team. We'll show you the full platform and answer any questions before you commit to anything.
