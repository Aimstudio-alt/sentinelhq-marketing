The problem

It's 7pm. You're still writing up the 2pm fall from memory.

Every shift makes evidence whether you capture it or not, the fall, the med round, the safeguarding note, the CQC prep that never quite gets done. Miss it and it's gone by handover. And the gap between what happened and what's on record is exactly what an inspection finds.

How it works

Speak it. The AI writes the report.

Compliance that actually gets done, because logging it is talking for twenty seconds, not a ten-minute form your team avoids at the end of a long shift.

01 / Speak

Say what happened, in plain words

Hold the tablet and describe the incident, complaint, safeguarding concern or medication error, where, who, how serious. No forms, no desk, no writing it up from memory later.

02 / Structure

AI fills the right record

CareSentinel picks the correct record type, fills the fields, assesses the risk and flags the obligations, a RIDDOR clock, a safeguarding threshold, a CQC notification. You review and save.

03 / Connect

The evidence builds itself

One voice note can log the incident, raise maintenance, draft the safeguarding referral and the RIDDOR report, all linked, and all feeding your live compliance score automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What is CareSentinel?

Voice-first compliance software for UK care homes, aligned to the CQC Single Assessment Framework. A staff member speaks what happened, a fall, a medication error, a safeguarding concern, and AI turns it into the right structured record with fields filled, risk assessed and reporting obligations flagged. Do it every shift and your inspection evidence builds itself.

How does voice reporting work?

Hold a phone or tablet and describe what happened in plain words. CareSentinel transcribes it, identifies the record type, fills the fields, assesses severity and flags obligations such as RIDDOR or a safeguarding referral. You review and save, logging a record takes around twenty seconds instead of a ten-minute form.

Does it cover the CQC Single Assessment Framework?

Yes. CareSentinel maps to all 34 quality statements across the five key questions, Safe, Effective, Caring, Responsive and Well-led, and gives you a live readiness score so you always know where you'd stand if an inspector arrived today.

What's included?

Every module from day one: incidents & RIDDOR, complaints, safeguarding, medications, training, CQC readiness, audits & inspections, documents & policies, COSHH register, supervisions, moving & handling and resident risk assessments, one platform, one live score, everything connected. New modules and features are added continuously at no extra charge.

How much does it cost?

£6 per bed per month, with a £198 monthly minimum and a 20-bed minimum, so a smaller home starts at £198 a month, and a 40-bed home is £240. Every module and all AI features are included, with unlimited staff records, no setup fees, free onboarding and cancel-anytime billing. Care groups of three or more homes get tailored group pricing.
