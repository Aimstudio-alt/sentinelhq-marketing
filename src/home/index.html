From the founder

Started by a golfer. Built with people who do the checks.

I'm Kevin. I've worked in tech and web development since 1997. The health and safety system at my previous club was hard to use and couldn't show the checks had actually been done. So I built ClubSentinel, and kept it simple enough for every member of staff to use every day. Once it was working, we realised plenty of other industries had the same problem: compliance software that's either too complicated or too generic to fit the job. So we took the same approach to junior sport, recruitment and care.

SentinelHQ is a small team based in North East England. I build the software, working alongside people who do compliance for a living, from recruitment compliance specialists to a golf club health and safety manager. They shape what each product does, so it works the way the job really works. When you get in touch, you speak to the people who built it. Every product since has started from the same question: when someone asks for the evidence, can you show it?

Kevin JagerFounder, SentinelHQ, on behalf of the team
