Technology

Smart tools that save you time — not a chatbot bolted on.

Most software has added a basic AI assistant as an afterthought. SentinelHQ uses technology at the specific points where it genuinely saves you time. A person always reviews and approves — the software handles the repetitive work.

01

Talk through your inspection

Walk an area and describe what you see out loud. The software turns your words into a complete, structured inspection record. No typing. No desk needed.

02

Chemical safety sheets, filled in automatically

Upload the supplier's safety sheet and the key information — hazards, protective equipment needed, what to do in an emergency — is read and saved for you.

03

No paperwork? Photograph the bottle.

No safety sheet to hand? Take a photo of the bottle or container label. The software identifies the chemical and fills in the record for you.

04

Accident reports, written for you

Describe what happened and the software suggests a corrective action — what caused it and what to do to prevent it happening again. You review and approve.

05

Complete an audit, get a professional report

Carry out an audit on your phone. A formatted, dated report is created automatically when you finish — ready to share with your board or show to a visiting inspector.

06

Ask questions, get answers from your own records

Want to know who is overdue for first-aid training? Just ask. The system gives a clear answer and shows exactly where it found the information.

CareSentinel · Inspections

Walk a room. Talk. The record writes itself.

Press record on your phone. Describe what you see as you walk. The software turns your words into a structured inspection — covering cleanliness, safety, equipment, environment and any actions needed — and saves it with a timestamp. No typing. No desk required.

  • Sectioned report: Cleanliness · Safety · Equipment · Environment · Actions
  • Actions are automatically picked out and added to your follow-up list
  • Works offline; syncs the moment you have signal

CareSentinel · Hazardous Substances

From a supplier's safety sheet to a full record in under a minute.

Drop in the supplier's safety sheet — two pages or sixteen. The software reads it and pulls out everything you need: the hazards, what protective equipment is needed, what to do in an emergency. It flags anything missing and files it against the right chemical.

  • Reads all the key information — hazards, protective equipment, storage and emergency guidance
  • Gives each chemical an overall safety score based on what it is, how it's stored and how it's used
  • Sends reminders when safety sheets need updating — 90, 30 and 7 days before they expire

CareSentinel · Incidents

Every accident checked against reporting rules the moment it's logged.

Staff describe what happened in plain language. The software works out how serious it is, checks whether it needs to be reported to the Health and Safety Executive, suggests the corrective action, and sends it to the right manager — with a full record throughout.

  • Seriousness is assessed using Health and Safety Executive guidelines
  • Automatically flags accidents that must be reported to the HSE
  • Suggests what caused the incident and how to prevent it happening again
