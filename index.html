ReferenceSentinel

Reference checking & workforce compliance, together.

Send reference requests; candidates nominate referees; referees complete structured questionnaires that intelligent AI scores, fraud-checks and turns into printable reports. Every worker tracked against a five-part readiness model.

  • Five-part readiness: ID · DBS · references · training · sign-off
  • Structured referee questionnaires, scored & fraud-checked
  • Add referees or upload off-portal references yourself
  • Accept short-coverage workers while chases run — date-stamped

CountyConsent

  • Parental consent with medical & emergency info in one record
